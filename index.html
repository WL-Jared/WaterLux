Why it keeps happening

Hard water doesn't go away on its own.

The minerals come from the ground your water travels through, so they arrive in every gallon, every day. Until they're removed where water enters your home, the spots, scale and wear keep coming back, and quietly cost you along the way.

Your water heater works harder

Scale settles inside the tank and coats the heating element, so it takes more energy to heat the same water.

You use more soap and detergent

Hard water fights lather, so it takes more shampoo, dish soap and laundry detergent to get things clean.

Fixtures wear out sooner

Showerheads clog, faucets crust over and glass doors cloud up, so they get replaced sooner than they should.

Cleaning takes longer

Spots, film and white buildup come back days after you scrub them away.

Appliances work harder

Dishwashers, washing machines and coffee makers collect scale inside where you can't see it.

Bottled water adds up

If you don't love the taste of your tap water, you're probably buying it by the case.

What fixes it

WaterLux Complete. Minerals and chlorine in one tank.

Most homes need two jobs done: softening the hard minerals and filtering out chlorine. WaterLux Complete does both in one central tank, with a brine tank alongside. Each system is custom built for your needs and family size.
