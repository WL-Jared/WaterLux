WaterLux Carbon

Whole-home carbon filtration for chlorine taste and smell.

What it does

WaterLux Carbon is a whole-home filter. All the water coming into your house passes through a deep bed of activated carbon, which grabs onto chlorine and the compounds behind off tastes and odors. You get cleaner-tasting, better-smelling water at every faucet and shower, not just the kitchen sink.

Best for homes with

  • Chlorine or bleach smell
  • Bad taste at the tap
  • Dry skin and hair after a shower
  • City water

Good to know: WaterLux Carbon does not soften water. If you also have spots and scale, WaterLux Complete does both jobs in one tank.

How it works

Simple, automatic, out of sight.

1

Water enters the top of the tank and flows down through the carbon bed.

2

Carbon attracts and holds chlorine and taste and odor compounds as water passes through.

3

Filtered water flows on to every tap in your home.

Maintenance

The carbon bed lasts for years and is replaced on a schedule based on your water use. We go over the schedule with you at install.

Questions

WaterLux Carbon FAQ

Will it remove the chlorine smell from my shower?

Yes. Because it filters all the water entering your home, it removes chlorine before it reaches your showers, not just your drinking water.

Does it work on chloramines?

Many cities now use chloramines instead of chlorine. The right carbon and enough contact time handle chloramines, and your water test tells us which size fits your home.

Is it the same as a pitcher filter?

No. A pitcher filters a small amount of drinking water. WaterLux Carbon filters every drop in the house, including showers, laundry and appliances.
