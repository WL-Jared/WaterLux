Air Injection System

Clears up iron, manganese and rotten egg smell.

What it does

An air injection system removes iron, manganese and hydrogen sulfide, the cause of orange stains, black stains and that rotten egg smell. It uses a pocket of air at the top of the tank to do the work, so there are no chemicals to add or handle.

Best for homes with

  • Orange or reddish-brown stains
  • Black or dark gray stains
  • Rotten egg smell
  • Metallic taste
  • Well water

Good to know: An air injection system does not soften water. Many well homes pair it with WaterLux Soft for complete filtration.

Photo: Air Injection System installedReplace with a real install photo

How it works

Simple, automatic, out of sight.

1

Water enters a pocket of air at the top of the tank.

2

Oxygen turns dissolved iron, manganese and sulfur into tiny particles.

3

The filter bed below traps those particles, and the system automatically backwashes to flush them out and refresh its air pocket.

Maintenance

The system backwashes on its own. The filter media lasts for years, and we check it during service visits.

Questions

Air Injection System FAQ

Why does my water smell like rotten eggs?

That smell comes from hydrogen sulfide gas, which is common in well water. An air injection system removes it.

What causes orange stains in my toilet and sink?

Orange and reddish-brown stains are iron in your water. Over time it also stains laundry and fixtures.

Do I need to add chemicals?

No. The system uses air, not chemicals, to do the work.
