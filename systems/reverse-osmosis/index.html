WaterLux RO Pro50

The cleanest-tasting water for drinking and cooking.

What it does

WaterLux RO Pro50 sits under your kitchen sink and gives you a dedicated faucet for drinking and cooking water. It pushes water through an extremely fine membrane that holds back most dissolved solids, so your water, coffee, tea and ice taste noticeably cleaner. Many families stop buying bottled water.

Best for homes with

  • Bad taste in drinking water
  • Buying bottled water
  • Better coffee, tea and ice
  • A dedicated drinking water faucet

Good to know: WaterLux RO Pro50 filters drinking water at one faucet. For softer, filtered water through the whole house, pair it with WaterLux Soft or WaterLux Complete.

How it works

Simple, automatic, out of sight.

1

Water passes through prefilters that catch sediment and chlorine.

2

Pressure pushes it through a very fine membrane that holds back most dissolved solids.

3

Clean water is stored in a small tank under the sink, ready at its own faucet.

Maintenance

The prefilters are changed periodically and the membrane lasts for years. We will let you know the schedule for your system.
