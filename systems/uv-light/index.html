WaterLux UV Pro9

Whole-home UV for well water. Up to 9 gallons a minute, with nothing added to your water.

Book My Free Water ConsultationTakes about 30 seconds to book. Free, up to 60 minutes, no obligation, no pressure.

What it does

WaterLux UV Pro9 is the last stage of a well water setup. Every gallon that comes into your home flows through a stainless steel chamber past a high-output ultraviolet lamp on its way to your taps. Nothing is added to your water, so it does not change how your water tastes or smells. A digital controller counts down the life of the lamp and sounds an alert when it is time for a new one, so you always know your system is working.

Best for homes with

  • Private wells
  • A final stage with no chemicals added
  • Pairing with WaterLux Complete
  • Low-maintenance setups

Good to know: UV does not remove hardness, iron or chlorine, and it works best on clear water. That is why it goes after equipment like WaterLux Complete. Your free water test tells us what should go in front of it.

How it works

Simple, automatic, out of sight.

1

Water enters the stainless steel chamber after your other equipment, like WaterLux Complete.

2

A high-output UV lamp, inside a clear quartz sleeve, treats the water as it flows past.

3

Your water continues on to every tap in the home, with nothing added.

4

The controller watches the lamp and counts down the days until it needs replacing.

Maintenance

Replace the UV lamp about once a year (every 9,000 hours) and clean the quartz sleeve at the same time. The controller counts down and beeps when it is time, and we will remind you too.

Questions

WaterLux UV Pro9 FAQ

Why is it called Pro9?

It handles up to 9 gallons a minute, enough for a whole home running showers, laundry and the kitchen at the same time.

Where does it go?

Where water enters your home, at the end of your well water setup. It goes after WaterLux Complete or any other equipment, so it works on clear water.

Will it change how my water tastes?

No. UV adds nothing to your water and does not change its taste or smell.

How do I know it is working?

The controller shows a countdown of the days left on the lamp. If the lamp ever stops working, it sounds an alarm.

What does it need each year?

A new UV lamp about once a year, and a quick cleaning of the quartz sleeve at the same time. We will remind you when it is time.

Does it use much power?

About 35 watts, similar to a single light bulb.
