Why well owners choose UV

City water is treated for you. Well water isn't.

If your home is on a private well, there's no water plant treating your water before it reaches your taps. Bacteria and other microorganisms can get into a well without changing how the water looks, smells or tastes, so there's no way to tell just by looking. That's why many well owners add UV as a final stage that works every time water runs.

No city treatment

A private well goes straight from the ground to your taps. What happens to it in between is up to you.

Clear is not the same as clean

Microorganisms don't change your water's look, smell or taste. Only a lab test can find them.

Always on

UV treats every gallon as it flows, around the clock, without you having to think about it.

What it does

WaterLux UV VH200 disinfects all the water coming into your home. As water flows through a stainless steel chamber, it passes a high-output lamp that gives off UV-C light at 254 nanometers. That light scrambles the DNA of microorganisms in the water, so they can no longer multiply or make anyone sick. Nothing is added to your water, so the taste and smell stay exactly the same.

What it's designed to inactivate

  • E. coli and fecal coliform bacteria
  • Cryptosporidium
  • Giardia
  • Other bacteria and viruses

Based on the manufacturer's rated UV dose of 30 mJ/cm² at 9 gallons a minute. UV does not remove chemicals, metals, hardness or sediment.

Best for homes with

  • Private wells
  • Typical homes with 1 to 3 bathrooms
  • Families who want water treated without chemicals
  • Pairing with WaterLux Complete

Good to know: UV disinfects, but it does not remove hardness, iron, sediment or chemicals. It also needs clear water to work, so a 5-micron sediment pre-filter goes in front of it, and on most wells it goes after WaterLux Complete. Your free water test tells us exactly what your well needs.

How it works

Simple, automatic, out of sight.

1

Pre-filtration is highly recommended, so it's clear when it reaches the UV chamber.

2

It flows through the stainless steel chamber, past a UV-C lamp at 254 nanometers.

3

The UV light inactivates microorganisms in the water, with nothing added.

4

Treated water continues on to every tap, while the controller keeps watch on the lamp.

Maintenance

Replace the UV lamp once a year (every 9,000 hours). Clean the quartz sleeve around the lamp as needed, and replace it every 2 years. The controller counts down and beeps when the lamp is due, and we will remind you too.

Questions

WaterLux UV VH200 FAQ

My well water looks and tastes fine. Do I need UV?

Maybe. Bacteria and other microorganisms don't change how water looks, smells or tastes, so clear water can still carry them. The only way to know is a lab test. UV gives you treatment that works every time water runs, between tests.

Will it change how my water tastes?

No. UV adds nothing to your water, so the taste and smell stay the same.

Does it remove iron, hardness or sediment?

No. UV only treats microorganisms. That's why it is often paired with WaterLux Complete, which handles hardness and chlorine before the water reaches the UV chamber.

Why does it need a pre-filter?

Particles in the water can shade microorganisms from the UV light. A 5-micron sediment pre-filter keeps the water clear, so the light reaches everything.

Is it big enough for my home?

It handles up to 9 gallons a minute, sized for typical homes with 1 to 3 bathrooms. Larger homes may need a bigger unit, and we'll size it at your free water test.

How do I know it is working?

The controller shows the days left on the lamp. It beeps when the lamp is due, and sounds an alarm if the lamp ever fails. When you hear it, call or text us at (636) 538-9009. We will come out, replace the bulb and do a full inspection of the unit.

What happens if the power goes out?

The lamp needs power to treat water, and it turns back on by itself when power returns. Water that flows during an outage is not treated, so ask us how to flush your lines afterward.

What upkeep does it need?

A new lamp once a year, and a quartz sleeve cleaning as needed with a new sleeve every 2 years. We will remind you when it is time.

Should I still test my well?

Yes. Testing your well once a year is still a good idea. UV handles microorganisms, and a test checks everything else.

Does it use much power?

About 35 watts, similar to a single light bulb.
