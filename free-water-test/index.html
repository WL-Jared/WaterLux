Free water test FAQ

Is the water test really free?

Yes. The water test and the consultation are both free, with no obligation to buy anything.

How long does it take?

Up to 60 minutes, depending on how in-depth you'd like to go. Russ will test your water, go over your results and answer every question, so you feel completely comfortable with your decision. There is never any rush.

Do you test well water?

Yes. We install on well water as well as city water.

Do I need to do anything to get ready?

Just make sure we can get to your kitchen sink. If you have a softener or filter already, it helps to know where it is.

Will I be pressured to buy something?

No. Our job is to show you what's in your water and explain your options. Whether you do anything about it is up to you.

What areas do you serve?

We serve about a one-hour radius of Lake St. Louis: St. Charles, St. Louis, Franklin and Lincoln Counties and the City of St. Louis in Missouri, plus St. Clair, Madison and Monroe Counties in Illinois. See our service areas page for details.
