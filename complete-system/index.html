WaterLux Complete

Softening and whole-home carbon filtration in one central tank. Every WaterLux Complete works the same way, and each system is custom built for your needs and family size. Available in Complete 9, Complete 10, Complete 12 and Complete 14. Your water test tells us which size fits your home.

Built in layers

One tank. Every layer does a job.

Water enters at the top and passes through each layer on its way to every faucet, shower and appliance in your home.

  • Top: coconut shell activated carbonCaptures chemicals that affect taste, smell and comfort.
  • Middle: softening resinPulls out the calcium and magnesium that make water hard.
  • Base: quartz media bedSupports the layers above and keeps water flowing evenly.
  • Alongside: brine tankAutomatically recharges the softening resin so it keeps working.

Why "complete"

Minerals and chlorine, handled together.

Most systems do one job. WaterLux Complete handles both the hard minerals and the chlorine in one unit.

Minerals

  • Hardness minerals (calcium and magnesium)
  • Scale on fixtures, glass and inside appliances
  • Spots on dishes and film in the shower

Chlorine

  • Chlorine and chloramines
  • Pool-like taste and smell at the tap
  • Dry-feeling skin and hair after a shower

What you'll notice

Better for your home. Better for your family.

Softer, filtered water touches everything in your house, from the pipes and appliances to your morning shower and the glass of water on the counter.

Your home

  • Less scale on faucets, showerheads, glass doors and inside your pipes
  • Appliances that work less hard. Water heaters, dishwashers and washing machines stay cleaner inside
  • Less soap and detergent. Soft water lathers easily, so a little goes further
  • Faster cleaning. No more scrubbing white film off fixtures every week
  • Spot-free dishes and glassware straight out of the dishwasher

Your family

  • Less chlorine in your shower. Carbon filtration reduces the chlorine that can leave skin and hair feeling dry
  • Softer-feeling skin and hair. Without hard minerals, soap rinses clean instead of leaving residue
  • Water that tastes clean from every tap, so it's easier to drink more of it
  • Softer towels and clothes that feel better and last longer
  • Peace of mind knowing what's in your water, because we test it first

WaterLux Complete FAQ

Is this two systems or one?

One. WaterLux Complete is a single central tank with layered media, plus a brine tank alongside. It handles both softening and filtration, and each system is custom built for your needs and family size.

What sizes does it come in?

Complete 9, Complete 10, Complete 12 and Complete 14. The number is the size, and your water test tells us which one fits your home.

Does it work on well water?

Yes. It can be installed on city water or a private well. We test your water first so the setup matches what your well needs.

What maintenance does it need?

The brine tank needs salt added periodically. We'll walk you through what to expect at install, and we're a phone call away for service.
