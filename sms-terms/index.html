SMS Terms

Last updated: October 2026

Program description

When you opt in through a form on WaterLuxit.com, WaterLux Installations, LLC will send you text messages about your request and service, including consultation scheduling, confirmations, reminders, follow-ups and service updates.

How you opt in

You opt in by checking the SMS consent box on one of our website forms and submitting it, or by texting us first. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your request and consultations.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details.

Opting out

You can cancel at any time. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You'll get one final message confirming you've been unsubscribed. To start again, reply START.

Help

Reply HELP for help, or call us at (636) 538-9009.

Carrier liability

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We do not sell, rent or share mobile phone numbers or text messaging opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy for more.
